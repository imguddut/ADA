Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming industries worldwide. Businesses leverage AI to optimize operations, enhance customer experiences, and drive innovation.

Machine Learning, a subset of AI, allows computers to learn from data and improve performance without being explicitly programmed. AI applications include speech recognition, image processing, predictive analytics, and autonomous systems.

The future of AI holds immense potential, with advancements in deep learning, neural networks, and natural language processing. As AI continues to evolve, ethical considerations and responsible deployment remain crucial.
